Match 3 slimes to pop them. Bigger matches leave runes behind.

Swap two runes into each other and they combine: lines cross, a line and a blast sweep three rows and three columns, two blasts go wide, and the orb turns a whole colour into copies of its partner.

How to play

Swap two neighbouring slimes to line up three or more of a colour. Finish the quest before the moves or the clock run out.

What blocks you

Frost
Chips away when you clear a match on top of it
Bramble
Locks its tile — work a match into it from around the side
Creeper vine
Locks its tile and spreads to another every few seconds. Cutting any of it resets the clock; cutting all of it stops the spread for the rest of the quest. It doesn't count toward the blocker goal — it's just in your way

Zeny and lives

Zeny is a wallet and survives losing. Clearing quickly pays a speed bonus. Run out of moves or time and you can spend zeny to stay in, or give up and lose one of your five lives. A life returns every 30 minutes. Unused moves become rune charges you carry to the next quest — pick which rune to place, then tap a slime.

Rune Fall

The slimes of Midgard have overrun the field. Line up three or more before the moves or the clock run out. Losing a quest costs one of your five lives, and a life returns every 30 minutes.
